"Longevity" has become one of the buzziest words in healthcare, but most of the conversation lives in a world of biohacking, wearables, and supplement stacks aimed at people who can already afford unlimited access to care. That framing leaves out the everyday workers who make up the backbone of the American workforce, and who stand to benefit the most from evidence-based prevention.
